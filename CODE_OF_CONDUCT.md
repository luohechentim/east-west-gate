# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Double Gate to be respectful, constructive, and safe for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Focus critique on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people.
- Be clear about uncertainty, limitations, and conflicts of interest.
- Welcome good-faith questions and different technical conclusions.
- Protect private prompts, customer data, credentials, and vulnerability details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, publication of another person's private information, and deliberately misleading security or review claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and discussions that violate this code. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub's private reporting facilities when available. Do not use public issues for security-sensitive reports; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
